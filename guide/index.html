01 · The Problem We Solved

The Real Enemy Isn't Discipline. It's Initiation Friction.

Every student coder knows the feeling: you have an assignment due tomorrow or a 40-video DSA playlist waiting. You want to work. But opening the browser, finding the tutorial link, navigating 5 nested Windows folders to open VS Code, and remembering what line of code you wrote yesterday feels like lifting a boulder.

Psychologists call this initiation friction and subtle executive dysfunction. In high-demand college routines, you also deal with time blindness — hours vanish while you fiddle with setups or worry about how much work is left.

The Senku Rule:
Senku does not track your life. Senku exists solely to push you past the first 120 seconds of resistance. Once you are in motion, your own brain takes over.

🚀

03 · Defeating Overwhelm

The 2-Minute Tiny First Step

Never write a task titled "Build entire frontend authentication system". Your brain will immediately resist and procrastinate.

Instead, every task in Senku gives you a prominent Tiny First Step prompt. Make it ridiculously small and easy:

  • "Open login.tsx and import useState"
  • "Watch just the first 4 minutes of Striver's Dynamic Programming video"
  • "Create the folder structure: /components /services"

When you click Start & Launch, that tiny first step is displayed right in front of you. Once you complete the first 2 minutes, momentum carries you forward effortlessly.

04 · Work Structure

Scheduled vs. Flexible Tasks

Real students don't work like corporate managers. Some tasks have hard deadlines; other projects are ongoing explorations. Senku separates them cleanly:

📅

Scheduled Tasks

For work that must happen at a specific hour — college lab submissions, live lectures, or designated study blocks. Senku triggers native desktop reminders and auto-launches resources at start time.

🌊

Flexible Tasks

For ongoing self-paced journeys without arbitrary calendar deadlines — like finishing a 100-problem LeetCode list or building a side project. No overdue pressure, ever.

05 · Analysis Paralysis Cure

🎲 Pick For Me

You have 7 flexible tasks on your backlog: learn Docker, practice trees, write that blog post, clean up your Git repo. You stare at the screen for 25 minutes unable to choose which one to do first.

Switch to Flexible (F) or use the Next Up Flexible tab, and click 🎲 Pick for me. Senku instantly rolls the dice and surfaces one task at random. Stop debating. Accept the challenge and start building.

06 · Zero Guilt

Soft Missed Tasks Conversion

Life happens. You fell asleep, college ran late, or an emergency popped up. Other productivity apps punish you with blaring red "OVERDUE" badges that make you feel like a failure.

⚠️ Missed is not Failure. It's just a reschedule.
In Senku, missed tasks are treated with zero shame. Open the Missed view (M) and convert any task with one click:
  • Make Flexible: Remove the time lock and keep it alive in your backlog.
  • Reschedule Today: Give yourself a clean start for this evening.
  • Mark Done: Completed offline? Archive it without guilt.
07 · Clean Mental Boundaries

Spaces: One Context at a Time

Don't mix your Computer Networks homework with your DSA practice or your React freelance gig.

Create a Space for each domain (e.g., DSA, College, WebDev, Senku). Each Space links directly to its own local Windows folder, its own task list, and its own file explorer.

  • Open in Explorer: Jump right into the Windows directory with a single click.
  • In-Folder Search: Type to filter files and folders in real time.
  • Fast Parent Navigation: Tap R anytime to navigate straight up to the parent directory (cd ..).
  • Smart Resource Binding: Attach PDFs, websites, repos, or notebooks straight to any task.
08 · Scratchpad

Rough Note (Shortcut: N)

During deep focus, you suddenly remember you need to buy milk, or you stumble upon a quick terminal command (git stash pop), or an idea strikes.

Don't open a bloated browser tab. Press N from anywhere in Senku. Your Rough Note appears instantly. Type your thought, hit save (or let it autosave), and jump right back to your task.

09 · Speed of Thought

Master Keyboard Shortcuts

True speed means keeping your hands on the keyboard. Senku is wired for single-key navigation:

Key Action Description
H Home View Jump back to your Next Up Launchpad and folder view.
T Today View Inspect all tasks scheduled for today.
U Upcoming View Browse tasks scheduled starting tomorrow and beyond.
F Flexible View View your ongoing, self-paced backlog.
M Missed View Inspect past scheduled tasks and softly reschedule them.
C Completed View Review your finished victories.
N Rough Note Open your immediate scratchpad for quick notes.
R Folder Up (cd ..) In the file explorer, instantly navigate up to the parent directory.
Ctrl + Alt + S Summon Senku Global hotkey to bring Senku to the front from anywhere in Windows.
Esc Close / Cancel Dismiss modals, escape forms, or return to Home.
10 · Sovereign & Offline

100% Local, Fast & Private

Your tasks, folders, and notes belong to you — not some remote cloud server.

  • Zero Account Creation: No email, no password, no tracking cookies.
  • Stored in Windows AppData: All data is stored purely on your local drive in standard JSON format.
  • Works 100% Offline: No Wi-Fi required. Open Senku in an airplane, train, or library with zero latency.
  • Zero Telemetry: We do not log what you study or what you build.
